Frequently asked questions

How do I reach the team? {{icon|chevron-down}}

Call (215) 743-0500 or email info@pgecomfort.com. The current website lists office hours of Monday–Friday 8 AM–6 PM and Saturday 9 AM–5 PM.

What's the difference between scheduling and sending an inquiry? {{icon|chevron-down}}

“Schedule Service” opens the scheduling calendar so you can request an appointment time. An inquiry sends a message through the company's existing contact form; it is not a confirmed appointment. Your appointment is confirmed only by the booking provider or directly by the team.

Do you offer financing? {{icon|chevron-down}}

The current website says financing is available with approved credit, for new system installations only (not service calls or maintenance contracts). Terms are set by the lender. Call the team to ask for details.

Where do these reviews come from? {{icon|chevron-down}}

They are reproduced from the Google review widget on the company's current website. See the source note above the credentials section for details.
